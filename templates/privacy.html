Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you enter, including the amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party services.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact the Spendly team using the contact details provided by the site owner.
